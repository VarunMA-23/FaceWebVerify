How verification works

Face embeddingInsightFace generates a 512-D vector locally — never stored on-chain.
Reverse-image discoveryConfigured providers search for matching public sources.
Source retrievalPages and images are fetched and classified by platform type.
Face comparisonCosine similarity compares your face against each candidate.
Evidence scoringA weighted 0–100 score combines multiple verification signals.
Cryptographic fingerprintCanonical JSON is hashed with SHA-256 for tamper evidence.
Blockchain attestationThe fingerprint is registered on Ethereum Sepolia testnet.
